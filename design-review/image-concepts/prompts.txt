DESIGN A — Pearl Precision
Use case: ui-mockup.
Create ONE exceptionally polished, high-end desktop WEBSITE DESIGN MOCKUP for the Korean industrial safety startup Safe-Lier (세이플라이어). It must look like the work of a world-class digital design studio with beautiful original art direction, not a stock website template or wireframe. Output a sharp tall image, approximately 1536 px wide by 2048 px high, showing the desktop website hero and the beginning of TWO subsequent sections. A flat, front-facing website screenshot, edge-to-edge canvas. No laptop/device/browser mockup frame, no presentation board, no multiple alternatives in one image.
Business accuracy: Safe-Lier is a PROTOTYPE in development, not a released or certified product. A fluorescent lime industrial safety vest with reflective silver strips has four compact black sensor/camera modules around front/back/left/right shoulders. Distance sensors detect approaching forklifts, trucks, excavators. Images go via Wi-Fi and distance/direction via BLE to the worker's own smartphone; ON-DEVICE AI judges collision risk; vest-mounted LEDs and audible warning alert the WORKER directly. No CCTV, control server, or vehicle-mounted equipment is required in the intended design. No cloud brain, no central surveillance interface, no smartwatch, no headset, no invented biometric health features. Product visual is a design concept, never a proven final manufactured product.
Brand colors: fluorescent yellow/lime, ink navy, white. Sophisticated, restrained, innovative, optimistic industrial safety. Avoid gloomy industrial danger, orange hazard aesthetics, cartoon characters, childish icons, gold/black luxury clichés, purple gradients, dashboard charts, generic rounded cards, fake customer logos, awards, certifications, invented metrics, fake sales claims. White and luminous light tones should dominate overall.
Typography: exquisitely spaced clean Korean modern typography and refined Latin typesetting. Text must be legible, correctly spelled, accurately composed in Korean; use only the supplied copy, don't invent extra paragraphs. Navy text, judicious lime highlights, editorial hierarchy, professional navigation and true website controls. Fine optical alignment, thoughtful whitespace. Small texts are still readable. Include "시제품 개발 중" visibly and discreetly; under product visual, "AI 생성 개발 개념 이미지". Include exact brand name "Safe-Lier" as a restrained typographic wordmark; no fabricated registered symbol or unrelated logo.
Header menu exact text: "솔루션" "기술" "팀 소개" and button "투자·제휴 문의".
CTA exact text "투자·제휴 문의 ↗" and secondary "작동 원리 보기".
Lower content can use these exact real feature labels: "360° 접근 감지" / "온디바이스 AI 판단" / "LED·경고음 직접 경고" / "간편한 현장 도입".
This is a visual concept review, not a requirement to cram an entire full site into a screenshot. Prioritize impeccable visual craft.
